## Penjelasan kolom

| Kolom | Nilai yang diperbolehkan | Sumber |
|---|---|---|
| **Research ID** | `UIAI-YYYY-NNN`, link ke `problems/` | diberikan `@maintainers` saat validasi |
| **Judul** | Judul masalah (boleh berubah; ID tidak) | Issue |
| **Cluster** | C1 AI Models, Data & Knowledge · C2 AI Systems, Software & Security · C3 Human-Centered & Responsible AI · C4 Applied AI for Human Flourishing; sekunder dalam kurung | label `cluster:*` |
| **Domain** | Education · Halal · Health · Food · Government · Business · Social Impact | field Mission Control |
| **Entry Door** | Problem · Dataset · Faculty Research · Course Project · Partner · Competition | field Mission Control |
| **Maturity** | Idea · TA Ready · Research Ready · Publication Ready · Impact Ready ([MST-03](../research-os/00-master/03-glossary.md)) | label `maturity:*` |
| **Priority** | P0-critical · P1-high · P2-medium · P3-low | label |
| **Problem Owner** | Orang/unit yang memiliki masalah (bukan mahasiswa yang mengusulkan, kecuali sama) | Issue |
| **Mentor** | Dosen kandidat/mentor tetap; `[isi]` bila belum ada | Issue / [AIR-03](../research-os/03-ai-research-ecosystem/03-faculty-research-alignment.md) |
| **Issue** | Nomor Issue `#NNN` | GitHub |
| **Status** | `ready` (siap diambil tim) · `active` (ada tim, sebut repo) · `blocked` · `done` (lolos G8) · `closed` (tidak dilanjutkan) | label `status:*` + gate |

Saat masalah diambil tim, tambahkan link repo `proj-YYYY-topic` pada kolom Status (`active → proj-2026-…`) dan pindahkan pelacakan gate ke Mission Control/leaderboard.
